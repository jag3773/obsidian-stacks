Every format your library comes in.

Not every library is a folder of PDFs. Stacks reads each file for an ISBN or DOI, looks it up, and files it beside its note. One reference holds every copy: the PDF, the EPUB, the chapter scan.

What a reference manager does, kept in plain notes.

Turn highlights into notesExtract annotations groups a PDF’s highlights by page. Re-running updates only Stacks’ block, never your writing.
Read on a tablet, bring it homeSet a note to reading and its PDF goes to your synced folder. When it returns changed, Stacks files it and pulls the highlights.
Find an open-access PDFChecks Unpaywall and the landing page, shows which copy it found, and attaches it only after you confirm.
Browse with BasesA starter base with a view for each reading status, unfiled references and anything missing a file.
Keep the library healthyA report of duplicate citekeys, broken links and orphaned files. Every repair shows a preview first.
Capture offline, sync anywhereA clip saved without a connection waits in the inbox and is retried later, without filing a duplicate.

COMING FROM ZOTERO?

Bring your library. Keep your notes.

Import from Zotero reads a Better BibTeX JSON export, shows you what it will do, adds properties to the notes you already have without touching their text, and can be undone.
